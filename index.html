Eine klare Oberfläche für komplexe Funk- und Weltraumwetterdaten.

Solar / Geomagnetik

Grundlage für viele HF- und VHF-Einschätzungen.

HF-Bedingungen

Qualitative Tag- und Nachtbewertung für die wichtigsten Amateurfunk-HF-Bandgruppen: 80–40 m, 30–20 m, 17–15 m und 12–10 m.

Schneller Überblick, welche Bänder gerade eher interessant sind.

VHF & VHF/Es-Watch

Anzeige von Aurora-, Sporadic-E- und EME-Indikatoren sowie ein kompakter Watch-Modus für 6 m, 4 m und 2 m mit Ampel, Watch Items und letztem Trigger.

Optional mit externen Kartenlinks wie DXMAPS und KC2G.

Juliusruh-Ionosonde

Anzeige ionosphärischer Messwerte wie foF2, MUFD, M(3000)F2, TEC, fmin, hmF2, foE, foEs und hEs inklusive Datenstand, Quelle und Qualitäts-/Vertrauenshinweisen.

Fachliche Datenbasis für Funk- und DX-Bewertungen.

◎

Local Radio Assessment

Standortbezogene lokale Einschätzung mit aktuellem oder manuellem Locator, Sonnenphase, Grayline, HF-/VHF-Score, Bandempfehlungen, Aurora-Hinweisen, Tagesausblick und optionalem Höhenprofil.

Standortzugriff bleibt optional.

↗

DX Path Assessment

Analyse eines Funkpfads zwischen Start und Ziel mit Entfernung, Azimut, Short-/Long-Path, Tages-/Nacht-/Grayline-Anteilen, Bandgruppen, Best-Now-Bewertung und optionalen stündlichen Fenstern.

Mit Auto- oder Experteneingaben für ionosphärische Parameter.

⌬

Solar- & Ionosonden-Trends

Verläufe wichtiger Solar-, geomagnetischer und Juliusruh-Ionosondenwerte machen steigende, fallende oder stabile Entwicklungen sichtbar.

Hilft, Bedingungen nicht nur punktuell, sondern im Verlauf zu beurteilen.

⇄

Ham Converter Tools

Lokale Tools für Frequenz, Wellenlänge, Leistung, Distanz, Temperatur, UTC, Maidenhead-Locator, Koordinaten, DMS, Bearing/Azimuth, Sonnenaufgang, Sonnenuntergang und Grayline.

Viele Umrechnungen laufen direkt lokal in der App.

✓

Lokale Alarme

Solar- und Geomagnetik-Alarme mit Schwellenwerten, Vorlagen, HF-Zeitplan, Statusanzeige und Android-Benachrichtigungen bei neuen Treffern.

Zum Beispiel Aurora möglich, 10-m-Öffnung wahrscheinlich oder M-Flare und höher.

What SolarScope offers

A clear interface for complex radio and space-weather data.

The following feature overview was derived from the app code and translated into a readable website section, while keeping the existing visual style intact.

☀

Solar / Geomagnetic

Overview of SFI, sunspots, A/K index, X-ray class, 304A, proton/electron flux, Bz, solar wind, aurora, geomagnetic field, signal noise level and flare probability.

A basis for many HF and VHF assessments.

≋

HF conditions

Qualitative day and night outlook for the key amateur-radio HF band groups: 80–40 m, 30–20 m, 17–15 m and 12–10 m.

A quick view of which bands may be worth watching.

⌁

VHF & VHF/Es Watch

Displays aurora, Sporadic-E and EME indicators plus a compact watch mode for 6 m, 4 m and 2 m with signal lights, watch items and last trigger.

Optionally with external map links such as DXMAPS and KC2G.

⌖

Juliusruh ionosonde

Shows ionospheric measurements such as foF2, MUFD, M(3000)F2, TEC, fmin, hmF2, foE, foEs and hEs, including data time, source and confidence notes.

A technical data basis for propagation and DX assessment.

◎

Local Radio Assessment

Location-based local assessment with current or manual locator, sun phase, grayline, HF/VHF score, band recommendations, aurora hints, daily outlook and optional elevation profile.

Location access remains optional.

↗

DX Path Assessment

Analyzes a radio path between start and target with distance, azimuth, short/long path, day/night/grayline fractions, band groups, best-now rating and optional hourly windows.

Supports automatic or expert ionospheric inputs.

⌬

Solar & ionosonde trends

History views for key solar, geomagnetic and Juliusruh ionosonde values make rising, falling or stable developments visible.

Useful for judging conditions over time, not only as a snapshot.

⇄

Ham Converter Tools

Local tools for frequency, wavelength, power, distance, temperature, UTC, Maidenhead locator, coordinates, DMS, bearing/azimuth, sunrise, sunset and grayline.

Many conversions run directly and locally in the app.

✓

Local alarms

Solar and geomagnetic alarms with thresholds, templates, HF schedule, status tracking and Android notifications for newly triggered matches.

Examples include aurora possible, likely 10 m opening or M-flare and higher.

3. General information about the app and scope

SolarScope is a mobile app used to display solar, geomagnetic and radio-related measurement or forecast data and to provide local converter and location-based features. These notices apply to the app itself. External websites and third-party providers are subject to their own privacy terms.

4. What data may be processed when using the app

Depending on the feature used, the app may process technical connection data for network requests, optional location data, search terms and coordinates for place search or reverse geocoding, user-entered content such as a callsign, locator or place name, and locally stored settings, cache and history data.

Based on the current state of the app, there are no user accounts, no login function, no user profiles, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs and no separate crash-reporting SDK in use.

5. App permissions

According to the current app setup, the app uses Internet access and may request location access when you actively use location-based features. Location access is feature-specific and is not required for general use of the app.

6. Local storage on the device

The app stores several items locally on the device, including an optional callsign, UI and convenience settings, local caches for solar and ionosonde data, local trend history, geocoding caches and Local Radio Assessment settings including a manual locator and the last locally saved current position.

According to the current app behavior, these local data are not automatically uploaded to the operator. Where no fixed retention period exists in the app logic, the data remain stored until overwritten, deleted in the app, or removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

7. Use of external data sources and third parties

The app directly requests content from external data sources. Depending on the feature used, these may include HAMQSL, Juliusruh / IAP / GIRO DIDBase, NOAA SWPC, Open-Meteo and Nominatim / OpenStreetMap. 8. Location data / altitude feature / current position

Location data are processed only if you actively use location-based features and grant the relevant permission. General use of the app is possible without location access. According to the current app behavior, location is not tracked in the background.

The altitude feature is not purely local. GPS or system values may be processed locally, but the app may also transmit current coordinates to Open-Meteo for online terrain elevation fallback or cross-checking. The app operator does not use this feature to create movement profiles.

9. Geocoding / place search / nearest place

For place search and reverse geocoding, the app may use Nominatim at nominatim.openstreetmap.org. Depending on the feature, place names, search terms, coordinates, language settings and technical request metadata may be transmitted. Results may be cached locally on the device.

Important: The app operator does not sell personal data. The app does not engage in data trading. Juliusruh, GIRO, HAMQSL, NOAA and comparable sources are used as specialist or measurement data sources for display, not as purchased personal user data. These notes apply to visits to this SolarScope website. The app privacy policy above applies to the use of the SolarScope app itself.

2. Hosting and technical access data

This page is built as a static website. If it is provided via GitHub Pages or a comparable static hosting provider, technically required access data may be processed when the website is opened, for example IP address, access time, requested file, browser and device information and technical log data.

The purpose is delivery of the website, technical stability, security and error analysis. Where personal data are involved, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

3. Language setting

On the first visit, the website detects the preferred browser language. If it is German, the page is shown in German. In all other cases English is used. If you manually change the language above, this choice may be stored locally in your browser so the selected language can be restored on your next visit.

4. No cookies, no tracking and no analytics in this file

The source code of this website does not include tracking scripts, analytics services, advertising networks or external fonts. In its current state, this website itself does not set cookies.

If external services, analytics tools, maps, videos, contact forms, newsletter features or cookies are added later, these privacy notes must be updated accordingly.

5. Email contact

If you contact us by email, the information you provide will be processed in order to answer your request. This may include your email address, your name, the message content and technical sending data.

6. No contact forms and no newsletter

This website currently does not contain a contact form or newsletter subscription. Contact is possible only via the email address provided.

Data Sources & Attribution

Data sources & notes

SolarScope uses external specialist data sources only to display scientific, technical and radio-related measurements or forecast data in the app. Data may be delayed, incomplete or temporarily unavailable.

  • HAMQSLExternal source for solar, geomagnetic, HF and VHF propagation data.
  • NOAA SWPCSpace-weather data, current values and historical solar/geomagnetic trends.
  • Juliusruh / IAPSpecialist source related to Juliusruh ionosonde values.
  • GIRO DIDBaseExternal specialist source for ionosonde data.
  • Open-MeteoMay be used for location-based values, especially elevation and elevation profile information.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMapMay be used for place search, reverse geocoding, map selection and OpenStreetMap attributions.
  • DXMAPS / KC2GExternal map links for additional radio and VHF/Es observation; content is not embedded or copied.
  • Local device storageStores settings, caches, history, callsign, locator and alarm data locally on the device.

Availability and accuracy note

SolarScope does not guarantee completeness, accuracy, timeliness or permanent availability of external data. External providers have their own privacy and usage terms. The operator does not sell personal data.

FAQ

Braucht SolarScope Standortzugriff?

Standortzugriff ist optional und nur für standortbezogene Funktionen erforderlich. Die allgemeine Nutzung der App ist auch ohne Standortzugriff möglich.

Welche Funktionen nutzen den Standort?

Zum Beispiel aktuelle Position, Locator, Local Radio Assessment, VHF/Es-Watch, Ortssuche, Reverse-Geocoding und Höhen- bzw. Höhenprofilfunktionen.

Funktionieren die Converter offline?

Viele Converter sind lokale Tools. Funktionen mit Ortssuche, Karten, aktuellen Standortdaten oder externen Datenquellen benötigen jedoch Internet oder Systemfreigaben.

Warum werden externe Datenquellen verwendet?

SolarScope nutzt externe Fachquellen, um wissenschaftliche, technische und funkbezogene Mess- oder Prognosedaten in der App anzuzeigen.

Werden personenbezogene Daten verkauft?

Nein. Nach aktueller Beschreibung verkauft der Betreiber keine personenbezogenen Daten und betreibt keinen Handel mit Nutzerdaten.

FAQ

Does SolarScope need location access?

Location access is optional and only required for location-based features. General use of the app is possible without location access.

Which features use location?

Examples include current position, locator, Local Radio Assessment, VHF/Es Watch, place search, reverse geocoding and elevation or elevation-profile features.

Do the converters work offline?

Many converters are local tools. Features with place search, maps, current location or external data sources require internet or system permissions.

Why are external data sources used?

SolarScope uses external specialist sources to display scientific, technical and radio-related measurements or forecast data in the app.

Are personal data sold?

No. According to the current description, the operator does not sell personal data and does not trade user data.
